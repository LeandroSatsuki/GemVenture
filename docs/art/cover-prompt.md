## Prompt utilizado

Create a premium wide horizontal key art banner for the indie game GEMVENTURE,
suited as a GitHub README cover and repository social preview. Reference 1 is the
exact miner character identity: squat chunky yellow mining helmet with square
headlamp, simple tan block face with two navy rectangular eyes, blue shirt, red
trousers, dark outlines. Reference 2 is art style / creature identity only, ignore
its UI and text. One polished composition, 2:1 horizontal landscape.

Large impeccably legible title GEMVENTURE in custom bold beveled block lettering
occupying upper left/center: GEM in cyan #5ee9e9 with blue #2890dc shadow, VENTURE
in warm sand #fbbe82 with ochre #de9751 shadow and dark navy #180d2f outline.
Only this text, no subtitle, labels, UI or fake badges.

On the right, same miner swings a chunky silver pickaxe in a dynamic airborne
mining pose above a breaking pink crystal cluster, rounded cube debris and small
solid rhythm-like arcs. Cave cutaway / semi-top-down isometric diorama across
lower half, winding descending corridor, four regular chunky crystal clusters
cyan/pink/yellow/lime, hint of small cute cyan slime and mushroom stone guardian
in distance, majestic giant hedgehog with crystal back peering from a broad wall
burrow as spectator, warmly lit wooden mine lift and small cozy refuge silhouette
in the upper background.

Clearly defined silhouettes, controlled negative space around title, dark
atmospheric cave background dominated by #180d2f #353658 #053239 with stepped flat
planes. Vibrant saturated harmonious HEPT32 solid colors only. One base and ONE
flat shadow tone per material; light upper left, chunky broad chamfers, smooth
organic bodies, thick clean navy contours, matte surfaces. No realistic texture,
gradients, blur, shine, neon haze, photorealism, excessive particles or additional
text. The poster should communicate rhythmic mining, colorful treasure,
escalating danger, a welcoming miner and a world worth descending into. Strong
commercial indie game key art, cohesive with the actual character rather than
unrelated concept redesign. Keep lettering and major subjects inside safe margins.
